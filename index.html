Care instructions

A few habits that protect the print and keep the washed black looking right.

  • Wash inside out. The print stays safe from rubbing against other clothes.
  • Cold water, 30°C at most. Keeps the color and the shape of the fabric.
  • Mild detergent, no bleach. Bleach damages both the color and the print.
  • Wash dark and light apart. Do not put the black and white tees in the same load.
  • Dry in the shade. High heat can shrink cotton and damage the print, so skip the hot dryer.
  • Iron inside out on low heat. Never iron directly on the print.
